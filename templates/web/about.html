About PetPal

PetPal was founded with a simple mission: to connect loving families with pets in need of homes. We believe every pet deserves a second chance at happiness, and every family deserves the joy that comes with pet companionship.

Our Mission

To make pet adoption accessible, transparent, and stress-free for both adopters and shelters. We strive to create a community where pets find their forever homes and families find their perfect companions.

How It Works

1

Browse Pets

Explore available pets from verified shelters. Filter by species, breed, age, and more.

2

Submit Request

Found your match? Submit an adoption request with a personal message to the shelter.

3

Welcome Home

Once approved, complete the adoption process and welcome your new family member home.

Our Values

  • Animal Welfare First: We partner only with shelters that meet high standards of animal care.
  • Transparency: Clear communication between adopters and shelters throughout the process.
  • Community: Building a supportive network of pet lovers, shelters, and advocates.
  • Accessibility: Making pet adoption easy for everyone, regardless of location or circumstances.
